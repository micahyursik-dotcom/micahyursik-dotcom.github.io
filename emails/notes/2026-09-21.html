this week's note

the mundane is where He is

Hi friend — welcome to the very first Mom-Note. This is what it'll always be: one verse, one shift from real mom life, one small reach to practice it, one short prayer, and a few little joys. Five minutes, once a week. That's it.

the verse
“He tends his flock like a shepherd: He gathers the lambs in his arms and carries them close to his heart; he gently leads those that have young.”
— Isaiah 40:11
the shift

This week I caught myself doing the thing again — wiping the counter, buckling the car seat, refilling the same cup for the fourth time — with my head somewhere else entirely. Planning. Replaying. Waiting for the "real" part of the day to start.

What if that wasn't the whole picture?

The pattern I'd slipped into: that the mundane stuff is the filler, and the meaningful stuff happens somewhere else, later, once the kids are down. That's not from the Lord. It's the world's math — big moments count, small ones don't.

The truth: Isaiah says He gently leads those that have young. Not the ones who finished the list. The ones with a toddler on their hip and a sink full of cups. He's not waiting for me at the end of the day. He's in the buckling and the wiping and the fourth refill. When I'm actually present in the ordinary, I'm not missing my life — I'm standing in the exact spot He said He'd be.

the reach · practice it today

Every time I notice I've drifted today, I'm taking three extra seconds. Not a whole mindfulness practice — three seconds. Feel the little hand in mine a beat longer. Actually look at the face I'm wiping. Let the ordinary moment be the moment. Try it with me and see what it does by dinner.

a short prayer
Jesus, we're not waiting for later anymore. Meet us in the small stuff today — the cups, the car seats, the crying at 4pm. Gently lead us. We're the ones with young, and you said you would. Amen.
