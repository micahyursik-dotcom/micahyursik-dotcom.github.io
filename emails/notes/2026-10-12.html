this week's note

for the mom who didn't get time to pray

the verse
“In the same way, the Spirit helps us in our weakness. We do not know what we ought to pray for, but the Spirit himself intercedes for us through wordless groans.”
— Romans 8:26
the shift

Confession: some days my "prayer time" is me riffing with Jesus in the car between the drop-off and the grocery store. Half-sentences. "Okay Lord, today — you know. Help." That's it. And for a long time I counted those days as days I didn't pray.

What if that wasn't the whole picture?

The lie I was agreeing with: that prayer only counts when it's long, quiet, and eloquent. That if I didn't sit down, He didn't hear me. That's not from the Lord. Romans 8 says the Spirit prays for us when we don't have words — He's not grading our sentences, He's finishing them.

The truth: "Pray without ceasing" (1 Thessalonians 5:17) was never a command to sit still for an hour. It's an invitation to never hang up. The half-sentence in the car is prayer. The "help" over the tantrum is prayer. The deep breath with His name in it is prayer. I've been praying way more than I gave myself credit for — and so have you.

the reach · practice it today

Today I'm praying one-sentence prayers, all day, on purpose. Out loud when I can. "Thank you for this." "Help me here." "I love you, be with her at school." No sitting down required. Count them if you want — I bet you hit 4 before lunch, and I bet you feel less alone by dinner.

a short prayer
Jesus, we're done hanging up. Take our half-sentences and finish them. Thank you that you hear the "help" and the deep breath and the car-line quietly-in-my-head prayers that get interrupted. We're yours, all day, in pieces. Amen.
