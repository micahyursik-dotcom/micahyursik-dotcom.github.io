this week's note

simple was enough today

the verse
“In vain you rise early and stay up late, toiling for food to eat”
— for he grants sleep to those he loves." — Psalm 127:2
the shift

This week I had a plan for a "good" day — the cute lunch, the craft, the clean kitchen, the calm mom. By 10am the craft was on the floor, lunch was crackers and cheese, and I was doing the thing where I'm still smiling but I'm silently grading myself. C-minus.

What if that wasn't the whole picture?

The pattern I'd conformed to: that a good day means everything done well, and if it's done simply, it's a lesser day and I'm a lesser mom. Nobody said that out loud. I just absorbed it. And it's not from the Lord — Psalm 127 literally calls the striving "in vain." He grants rest to the ones He loves. The love isn't the reward for finishing. The love is the starting point.

The truth: crackers and cheese was lunch. The kitchen at 70% was clean enough. My kids didn't want a Pinterest day; they wanted a mom who wasn't grading herself. Simple solutions are allowed to be the solution — and I'm allowed to be happy about it, not just tolerate it.

the reach · practice it today

Today, when a task is "good enough," I'm moving to the next one. No redo. No hovering. Simple solution, done, on to the next thing — and I'm going to let myself be happy about the simple version, not secretly disappointed. If you catch yourself re-wiping something, that's your cue. Walk away and pick a kid up instead.

a short prayer
Jesus, we're putting down the grade book. Thank you that your love came first, before we did anything. Let simple be enough in our houses today, and let us actually enjoy it. Amen.
